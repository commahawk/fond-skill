# Nonna's Ragù

## Brown the Meat [phase=cook section=main duration=10 attention_level=high]
Brown the @beef{1 lb ground beef} in the big pot with a little @oil{2 tbsp olive oil} until no pink shows.

## Add the Tomatoes [phase=cook section=main duration=3 attention_level=full depends_on=[brown-the-meat]]
Crush the @tomatoes{2 cans whole tomatoes} in with your hands and add a pinch of @salt{salt}.

## Simmer the Sauce [phase=cook section=main duration=90 attention_level=low depends_on=[add-the-tomatoes]]
Simmer low and slow, stirring now and then, until thick, ~simmer{90%min}.
