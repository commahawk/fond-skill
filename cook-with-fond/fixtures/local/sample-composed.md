# Weeknight Dal

## Rinse the Lentils [phase=prep section=main duration=2 attention_level=full]
Rinse the @lentils{1 cup red lentils} under cold water until it runs clear.

## Simmer the Dal [phase=cook section=main duration=22 attention_level=low depends_on=[rinse-the-lentils]]
Simmer the lentils in @water{3 cups water} with a pinch of salt until soft and falling apart, ~simmer{20%min}.

## Season and Serve [phase=serve section=main duration=2 attention_level=full depends_on=[simmer-the-dal]]
Taste for salt and serve hot.
